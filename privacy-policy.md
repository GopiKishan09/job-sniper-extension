# Privacy Policy

**Job Sniper Chrome Extension**

Last updated: September 2026

---

## 🔍 Overview

Job Sniper is a Chrome extension designed to enhance the LinkedIn job search experience by providing features like auto-refresh and improved browsing.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

---

## 📊 Data Collection

This extension **does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data**.

We do not:

* Track user activity
* Collect personal information
* Send data to external servers

---

## ⚙️ Permissions Usage

The extension requires the following permissions:

* **tabs**
  Used to read and update the LinkedIn Jobs tab's URL (to apply filters) and to reload that tab when auto-refresh is enabled.

* **storage**
  Used to save the auto-refresh setting (on/off, interval and target tab) and the Hide Promoted / Hide Viewed settings locally on your device.

* **alarms**
  Used to schedule automatic refresh intervals.

* **notifications**
  Used to notify users when auto-refresh stops (for example, when the LinkedIn tab is closed).

---

## 🌐 Host Permissions

* Access is limited to:
  `https://www.linkedin.com/*`

This is required to enhance the LinkedIn job browsing experience. On LinkedIn Jobs pages, the extension reads the labels on job cards (such as "Promoted" or "Viewed") only to hide those cards when you turn that option on. Nothing it reads leaves your browser.

---

## 🔐 Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data leakage from this extension.

---

## ❗ Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking tools, or external APIs.
